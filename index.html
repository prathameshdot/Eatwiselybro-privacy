EatWisely Bro Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how EatWisely Bro ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile app and/or web services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect certain information to operate and improve the app, including:

1.1. Automatically Collected Data

  • Device identifiers (e.g., device ID, OS version, model).
  • Usage data (e.g., app usage, crashes, feature usage, timestamps).
  • Analytics data to help us improve features and performance.

1.2. User-Provided Data

  • Profile data (name, email) if you create an account or sign in.
  • Food selections, scan history, and dietary preferences.
  • Any content you choose to share (e.g., feedback, support messages).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve EatWisely Bro.
  • Personalize recommendations and food compatibility insights.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important updates.
  • Monitor and troubleshoot performance and security issues.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share data with trusted third parties that help us operate the app, including:

  • Cloud services (e.g., Firebase, Firestore, hosting, storage).
  • Analytics providers to help improve the app experience.
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights.

4. Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies in the web version to:

  • Remember user preferences.
  • Maintain sessions (for signed-in users).
  • Measure usage and performance.

In the mobile app, data is stored locally (e.g., shared preferences, local cache) to support offline use.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Request access to the data we’ve collected about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.

6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

7. Children’s Privacy

EatWisely Bro is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
